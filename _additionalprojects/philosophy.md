For my philsophy minor, I took a graduate level philospphy class titled "Plato onb Pleasure." In this class we anaylzed several of Plato's writings on pleasure and studied topics including if pleasures is good, whether the best life is the most pleasant, the nature of pleasure, whether pleasures can be false.

My final thesis is as follows: Through the Athenian’s dialogue in Laws II and Laws V and the natures of virtue and pleasure, Plato demonstrates that pursuing a virtuous life brings about more pleasure in one’s life than a life where pleasure is the main object of pursuit. This argument is supported by objectivity of virtue and forms of the good, avoidance of false pleasures, complete satisfaction of the soul, and the blessings from the gods all associated with the following of a virtuous life. 

TLDR: Plato and I agree that the virtuous life ought to be lived for the sake of itself and it is consequently the most pleasant.
