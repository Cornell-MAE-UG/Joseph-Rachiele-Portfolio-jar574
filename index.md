## About Me

I am an undergraduate senior studying Mechanical Engineering at Cornell University. This upcoming spring semester, I plan to finish my major requirements as well as my minor requirments while starting courses for a Master's in Mechanical Engineering. My expected undergraduate grduation is Spring 2026 and my expected Master's Degree graduation is Spring 2027.

My passion for engineering stems from  my childhood obsession with Star Wars and my desire to have a tangible, postivie impact on the world. Having a good background in math and science, I wished to pursue engineering in college to both work with my strengths and stimulate my interest. 

After deciding on mechanical engineering during my sophomore year, my undergraduate classes have been focused on aerospace engineering, while my undergraduate internships have given me a good background in project management, contracting, construction, and research.

Besides academics, I am captain of the Cornell Track and Field team. During my freshman year, I trained diligently to earn a walk-on spot as a javelin thrower. Three years later, I am now the captain of the team and have scored in multiple conference championships. 

My experiences thus far, both inside and outside of the classroom, have allowed me to develop into a well-rounded undergraduate engineer, excited to contribute to the engineering industry.
